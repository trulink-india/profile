**TRULINK Networking Solutions** is a Gurgaon-based IT infrastructure company for homes, offices, shops, and commercial sites. We design, supply, install, and maintain the networks and security systems that keep a building connected and protected.

The work is end-to-end: product selection, structured cabling, configuration, handover, and after-sales support. The standard is simple — the right solution, professional installation, and service that still answers the phone after the job is done.

> Professional installation, maintenance, and troubleshooting for networking, servers, and advanced security systems.

## What we do

| Networking | Security |
| --- | --- |
| **Server installation** — setup, configuration, and maintenance for reliable data and application hosting | **CCTV** — high-resolution cameras with remote monitoring and mobile access |
| **Office networking** — LAN/WAN design, switch configuration, and network optimisation | **Biometric access** — fingerprint, facial recognition, and card-based control |
| **Structured cabling** — CAT6, CAT6A, and fibre for data, voice, and video | **Video door phones** — visitor management for homes and commercial premises |

Additional field work includes fibre splicing and termination, wireless point-to-point links, network rack build-outs, intercom setup, IP camera systems, and annual maintenance contracts (AMC).

---

## How an engagement runs

1. **Site visit & requirement** — understand the floor, users, cameras, and access points that actually matter.
2. **Design & quotation** — bill of materials, layout, and a clear scope before anything is pulled through a ceiling.
3. **Supply & installation** — certified technicians, labelled cabling, and equipment from established brands.
4. **Configuration & handover** — Wi-Fi, firewall, NVR, biometric, and mobile apps documented for the people who will use them.
5. **Support & AMC** — troubleshooting, repairs, and 24/7 support when the network or cameras cannot wait until morning.
